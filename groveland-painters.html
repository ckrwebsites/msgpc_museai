Groveland Painting Questions

My Groveland home is new — why repaint so soon?

Builder-grade paint is thin and fades quickly in Florida sun. Repainting within the first few years with quality coatings protects your siding and stucco and noticeably upgrades the look.

Do you work with Groveland HOAs on color choices?

Yes. Many newer Groveland communities have approved palettes. Your HOA is usually very specific about which colors can go where on the exterior — we help you pick from the approved palette and handle the approval as part of your project.

What does a free estimate include?

We visit your home, measure, look at the condition of your current paint, discuss colors and products, and give you an exact written price — no pressure, no obligation.

Do you paint cabinets in Groveland?

We do — custom cabinet painting is one of our specialties. It's the fastest way to transform a kitchen without the cost of new cabinets.
